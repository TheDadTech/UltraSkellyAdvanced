UltraSkellyAdvanced 0.30.1-beta1 - EXPERIMENTAL Pi 3B support
Hardware validation is pending. This is not the stable image.

Allow 5-10 minutes on the first boot. Use a spare SD card.
Ethernet: connect to a router with DHCP, then read the monitor address.
Wi-Fi: use the monitor/keyboard, or join UltraSkellyAdvanced-Setup
with password dadtech1 and open http://192.168.4.1.
After connecting to home Wi-Fi or Ethernet, open the displayed IP
or http://usa-controller.local from your phone/computer.

Local Qwen does not preload on Pi 3B/3B+. SSH remains off.
If setup fails, save USA-BETA-DIAGNOSTICS.txt from this boot partition.
It appears automatically after boot; review network names before sharing.
Do not reflash before preserving that report.
